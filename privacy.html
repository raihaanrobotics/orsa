Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2, 2026

Orsa is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Wear OS application collects, uses, and safeguards your health and biometric information.

1. 100% On-Device & Offline Architecture

Orsa is built as an offline-first application. All processing, biometric algorithms, trend evaluations, and score calculations (including Heart Rate Variability, Resting Heart Rate, and Sleep Restorativeness) occur entirely on your local Wear OS smartwatch and connected Android device.

  • We do not operate external servers to collect your personal health metrics.
  • Your health data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export a CSV log to your own local device storage.

2. Information We Access via Health Connect & Permissions

To compute your daily readiness and body fuel scores, Orsa requests read-only access to specific health permissions via Google Health Connect and Wear OS system APIs:

  • Heart Rate Data: Used to evaluate sleeping resting heart rate (RHR) and RHR Nadir timing.
  • Heart Rate Variability (HRV / RMSSD): Used to calculate autonomic nervous system recovery status.
  • Sleep Sessions & Sleep Stages: Used to calculate total duration, deep sleep, and REM sleep restorativeness ratios.
  • Body Sensor & Foreground Permissions: Used to perform background data syncing between Health Connect, local storage, tiles, and complications.

3. Limited Use Disclosure (Google Play & Health Connect Policy)

Orsa strictly adheres to the Google Health Connect Developer Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • Specific Use Only: Health Connect data is accessed solely to provide and improve user-facing features (calculating recovery scores, trends, tiles, and complications).
  • No Data Transfers: We do not transfer, sell, or disclose Health Connect or sensor data to any third parties, advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.
  • No Advertising: Health and biometric data is never used for advertising purposes.
  • No Human Inspection: No human reads or inspects your Health Connect data.
  • No AI Training: Health Connect data is never used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models outside your local hardware.

4. Data Storage & Retention

  • Local Processing: All processed biometric records are handled and maintained securely on your local device storage.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete all application data at any time by clearing the app data in Android system settings or by uninstalling the Orsa app from your watch and phone.

5. Third-Party Services

Orsa does not integrate third-party tracking scripts, advertising SDKs, or external analytics frameworks.

  • Google Play In-App Billing: If you purchase a premium license, payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play Billing services subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Orsa does not store or access your payment card information.

6. Medical Disclaimer

Orsa is designed for general wellness, fitness tracking, and personal awareness only. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition or disease.

7. Children's Privacy

Orsa does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions).

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
