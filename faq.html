Everything you need to know about the Orsa closed beta, biometric data security, device compatibility, and energy scoring engine.

01 What is Orsa and how does it calculate my body fuel score?

Orsa evaluates your nightly resting heart rate (RHR) trends and heart rate variability (HRV) ratios to generate a single 0–100% body fuel score. This tells you precisely when your body is fully recharged to train hard or when it requires extra rest. Tailored insights are provided to guide you.

02 How do I use Orsa and interpret my results to get actionable insights?

Wear your watch consistently while sleeping to let Orsa build your nightly baseline. Check your score first thing in the morning: a high score (75%–100%) indicates your autonomic nervous system is recovered and ready for high strain, while a lower score signals a need for active recovery or rest.

03 Do I need a companion phone app to view my stats?

No. Orsa operates completely on your wrist as a standalone Wear OS application. You can view interactive 7-day trajectory graphs and 30-day historical bar charts directly on your watch screen without needing an active phone companion app open.

04 What are the exact device and OS requirements?

Orsa requires Wear OS 4.0 or newer. Compatible hardware includes Samsung Galaxy Watch 4, 5, 6, 7+, FE, Classic & Ultra series, Google Pixel Watch 1, 2, 3+, OnePlus Watch 2+, Xiaomi Watch 2+, and OPPO Watch X. It also relies on an Android phone running Google Health Connect for zero-cloud background synchronization.

05 Is my health and biometric data secure and private?

Yes, 100% private. Your personal biometric data never uploads to external cloud servers. All metric computations run securely on your own device, and you maintain full ownership with options to export your logs to local CSV storage at any time.

06 Can I display Orsa body fuel scores directly on my watch face?

Yes. Orsa includes built-in Wear OS watch face complications and dedicated glanceable Tiles. You can monitor your current body fuel percentage and daily recovery trend instantly without opening the full application.

07 What visual customization options does Orsa offer?

Orsa supports custom aesthetic color themes on-device. You can personalize your watch UI, trajectory graphs, and complication accents with high-contrast ambient palettes tailored for AMOLED displays.

08 Does Orsa drain smartwatch battery life in the background?

No. Orsa is optimized for ultra-low energy consumption. Instead of running continuous background sensor polling, it pulls consolidated biometric intervals directly from Google Health Connect during natural wake-up events.

09 How does the Closed Beta testing invitation work?

When you submit your Play Store Gmail address on our landing page, we will manually whitelist your email for Google Play closed testing. You will receive an official Google testing link and setup instructions directly in your inbox.

10 Is Orsa a certified medical device?

No. Orsa is created purely for general health, wellness, and fitness tracking awareness. It is not a medical device and should not be used to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical conditions.
