Setup & User Guide

Follow this complete 6-step walkthrough to join our testing pool, configure Health Connect across your specific phone brand, build your baseline, and submit feedback.

01

Join & Accept Opt-In

Submit your Google Play Store Gmail address on the landing page form. Once our team manually whitelists your account, open the official Google testing invite link sent to your inbox and select "Become a Tester".

02

Install on Wear OS & Phone

Download Orsa directly from the Google Play Store on your Wear OS 4+ smartwatch. Make sure to open the companion app on your phone to trigger health permission prompts.

03

Configure Health Connect By Phone Brand

Orsa relies on Google Health Connect for zero-cloud local syncing. Ensure Health Connect is installed via Google Play (built-in on Android 14+), then enable sync permissions for your device brand:

Samsung Galaxy

Open Samsung Health → Settings → Health Connect → Turn ON all metrics (Heart Rate, HRV, Sleep) and allow Orsa read access.

Google Pixel

Open Fitbit / Google Fit → Settings → Health Connect → Grant Orsa permission to access Sleep Stages, Resting Heart Rate, and HRV.

Xiaomi & POCO

Open Mi Fitness → Profile → Connected Apps → Enable Health Connect sync, then allow Orsa read permissions in Phone Settings → Privacy → Health Connect.

OnePlus & OPPO

Open OHealth App → Profile → Data Sharing / Health Connect → Link Health Connect and allow Orsa permission to read biometric intervals.

Other Android Devices (Motorola, Nothing, Vivo, RealMe, Huawei)

Ensure the Google Health Connect app is installed from Play Store. Go to Phone Settings → Security & Privacy → Privacy → Health Connect → App Permissions → Select Orsa and switch Allow All to ON.

04

Sleep & Build Your Baseline

Wear your watch consistently while sleeping for 3 to 5 consecutive nights. Orsa requires this initial timeframe to establish your personal Resting Heart Rate (RHR) and Heart Rate Variability (HRV) baseline averages.

05

Check Daily Recovery & Tiles

Wake up and check your Body Fuel Score (0–100%) instantly on your watch face, glanceable Tiles, or complications to evaluate whether your nervous system is ready for high strain or requires active rest.

06

Send Feedback & Report Bugs

Encountered an issue or have suggestions for new features? You can submit private beta feedback directly through the Google Play Store listing page or email our development team at orsahealth@gmail.com. Your input helps shape the official public release!

Orsa is created for general health and energy awareness. Not a medical device.
