Setup & User Guide

Follow this complete step-by-step guide to join the beta, install Orsa on your smartwatch, configure Health Connect, and build your baseline.

01

Join & Accept Opt-In via Laptop

Open the official Google Play Opt-In link in your web browser. Log in using the exact same Google account logged into your smartwatch and tap "Become a Tester".

Precaution: Accounts must match between your store login and watch so the app becomes available for download.
02

Install on Your Smartwatch

After tapping "Become a Tester", click the blue "Download it on Google Play" link on the confirmation page. Use the device dropdown menu to push and install Orsa directly to your Wear OS watch.

Pro Tip: Using the dropdown arrow next to the button install on more devices makes remote watch installation effortless.
03

Configure Health Connect (One-Time Setup)

Orsa reads your health metrics locally via Google Health Connect. Open Orsa on your watch first and tap "Grant Access" on the popup prompt.

Ensure your watch companion app is writing data properly by selecting your phone brand below:

2. Ensure Samsung Health has Write Access enabled for Heart Rate, HRV, and Sleep data.

2. Tap App Permissions → Select Fitbit / Google Fit and confirm write access is active for sleep and heart rate.

2. Confirm Mi Fitness is writing Heart Rate, Sleep, and HRV to Health Connect.

04

Sleep & Build Your Baseline

Once permissions are granted, Orsa automatically begins analyzing your overnight metrics.

Calibration Mode (Days 1–7): Wear your watch consistently while sleeping. Orsa spends its first few days establishing your personal baseline variances, displaying a CALIBRATING (X/7) status on screen.

When it MUST be worn: Fasten your smartwatch snugly about one finger's width above your wrist bone throughout the entire night while sleeping. Overnight heart rate and HRV are mandatory for accurate daily scoring.

05

Check Daily Recovery & Tiles

Full Insights: Once calibrated, your full daily Body Fuel Scores (0–100%), 7-day trajectories, and smart recovery insights will generate automatically every morning upon waking up! Access them instantly on your watch face, glanceable Tiles, or complications.

Pro Tip: Swipe left on your main watch face to add the Orsa Body Fuel Tile for one-tap access to your daily recovery score.
06

Send Feedback & Report Bugs

Encountered an issue or have suggestions for new features? You can submit private beta feedback directly through the Google Play Store listing page or email us at orsahealth@gmail.com. Your input helps shape the official public release!
