Setup & User Guide

Follow this complete 6-step walkthrough to join our testing pool, configure Health Connect across your specific phone brand, build your baseline, and submit feedback.

01

Join & Accept Opt-In

Open the official Google Play Opt-In Link in any browser on your phone or computer. Log in with the exact Google Account connected to your smartwatch and select "Become a Tester".

Precaution: Make sure you opt in using the exact same Gmail address logged into the Google Play Store on your Wear OS watch, otherwise the listing will appear as unavailable.
02

Install on Wear OS

After tapping "Become a Tester", click the blue "Download it on Google Play" link on the confirmation page to open the store listing. Install Orsa on your Wear OS 4+ smartwatch using either method:

  • Via Phone/Web: Open the Play Store page → tap the dropdown arrow next to the install button → select your smartwatch and push install.
  • From your Watch: Open Google Play Store directly on your watch → search for "Orsa" → tap Install.
Phone Compatibility Notice: Because Orsa is a standalone watch application, checking the link on your phone browser may show "Your device isn't compatible with this version". Simply use the device selector dropdown or open the Play Store right on your watch to bypass this.
03

Grant Health Connect Permissions (One-Time Setup)

To compute your daily Body Fuel Score and HRV recovery metrics accurately, Orsa needs access to read your sleep and heart rate data via Health Connect. Open Orsa on your watch for the first time, tap "Grant Permissions", and select "Allow All" (or toggle on access for Heart Rate, Sleep, and Heart Rate Variability).

Ensure sync permissions are enabled for your specific phone brand below:

1. Open Samsung Health on your phone → tap the 3 dots (top right) → Settings → Health Connect.

2. Ensure Samsung Health is writing Heart Rate, HRV, and Sleep data to Health Connect.

3. Select Orsa under App Permissions and grant Allow All read access.

Precaution: Disable "Pause app activity if unused" for Samsung Health to prevent overnight sync delays.

1. Open Phone Settings → Security & Privacy → Privacy → Health Connect.

2. Tap App Permissions → Select Orsa.

3. Switch permission access for Sleep Stages, Resting Heart Rate, and HRV to Allow All.

Guidance: Ensure Fitbit or Google Fit is configured as the primary data writer inside Health Connect.

1. Open Mi Fitness → Profile → Connected Apps → Enable Health Connect sync.

2. Navigate to Phone Settings → Privacy → Health Connect → App Permissions → Select Orsa.

3. Enable read permissions for Heart Rate, Sleep, and HRV.

Precaution (MIUI/HyperOS): Set Mi Fitness battery saver setting to "No restrictions" to allow background biometric sync.

1. Open OHealth App → Profile → Data Sharing / Health Connect → Link Health Connect.

2. Go to Phone Settings → Privacy → Health Connect → App Permissions → Select Orsa.

3. Allow Orsa permission to read biometric intervals.

Precaution (ColorOS/OxygenOS): Turn off Auto-Optimize for OHealth under Battery settings.

1. Ensure Google Health Connect is installed from the Play Store.

2. Go to Phone Settings → Security & Privacy → Privacy → Health Connect → App Permissions → Select Orsa.

3. Switch Allow All permissions to ON.

Guidance: Ensure your primary watch companion app (e.g., Health Connect, Google Fit) has background data permission enabled.

04

Sleep & Build Your Baseline

Once permissions are granted, Orsa automatically begins analyzing your overnight metrics.

Calibration Mode (Days 1–7): Wear your watch consistently while sleeping. Orsa spends its first few days establishing your personal baseline variances, displaying a CALIBRATING (X/7) status on screen.

When it MUST be worn: Fasten your smartwatch snugly about one finger's width above your wrist bone throughout the entire night while sleeping. Overnight heart rate and HRV are mandatory for accurate daily scoring.

05

Check Daily Recovery & Tiles

Full Insights: Once calibrated, your full daily Body Fuel Scores (0–100%), 7-day trajectories, and smart recovery insights will generate automatically every morning upon waking up! Access them instantly on your watch face, glanceable Tiles, or complications.

Pro Tip: Swipe left on your main watch face to add the Orsa Body Fuel Tile for one-tap access to your daily recovery score.
06

Send Feedback & Report Bugs

Encountered an issue or have suggestions for new features? You can submit private beta feedback directly through the Google Play Store listing page or email us at orsahealth@gmail.com. Your input helps shape the official public release!
